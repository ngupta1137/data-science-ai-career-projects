# Decision memo: can this extractor process invoices from a new customer's format without review?

Generated by `python -m extraction_eval.report` from `results/`. Do not edit by hand.

## The question
Can the extractor be switched on for a customer whose invoice layout was not in the training data, with no human checking the output?

## The rule, set before results were seen
Pass only if all three hold on the test sets for the headline extractor, which is picked on dev as the one with the higher share of documents with all five fields correct: (1) on documents from templates seen in training, the lower 95% bound on that share is at least 0.90; (2) on documents from the two unseen templates, pooled, the lower 95% bound is at least 0.70; (3) on seen templates, the 95% paired interval on that share minus the share of the first-match baseline has a lower bound above zero. The 0.90 and 0.70 bars are assumptions fixed before results.

## The call: FAIL
Headline extractor: span_ranker.
- seen_doc_level_lower_bound_at_least_floor: True
- unseen_doc_level_lower_bound_at_least_floor: False
- beats_first_match_on_seen: True

Documents fully right on seen templates: 1.000 (0.997 to 1.000). On the two unseen templates pooled: 0.313 (0.288 to 0.340). Against the first-match baseline on seen templates: +1.000 (+1.000 to +1.000).

## Why the seen-template number is not the answer
It is 1.000, which looks finished. A random split of documents from templates the model has seen measures how well it memorised those templates. Holding one template out and training on the other two gives a mean of 0.553. The generated documents are also far cleaner than real ones, so even the seen number is an upper bound on what real invoices would give.

## The danger is silent errors, not low accuracy
On unseen templates the headline returned 715 wrong values, and 715 of them are valid dates, amounts or currencies that pass any schema check. The hand-written rules returned nothing on unseen templates instead of guessing (abstain rate 1.00), which is the safer failure even though its accuracy is lower. A threshold on confidence was set once (0.5) and not tuned; it did not stop the wrong values.

## What would work
Label a few documents from each new template and retrain. In the post hoc study, 10 labelled documents from template E took the share of fully right documents to 1.000. More labelled documents from seen templates did nothing for unseen ones (see the learning curve). Until a template has labels, route its documents to review.

## What a wrong call costs
A wrong total or date that parses is posted to a ledger without anyone seeing it. The cost per error is an assumption this project does not make.

## Where not to trust it
Any template whose label words or layout differ from training. Anything with scan noise: at the highest noise rate no span extractor can recover the ceiling shown in the model card. Anything outside this generator.

## Not done
No LLM extractor, no layout or image model, no line-item extraction, no calibrated confidence, no real invoices. The LLM-with-schema run is designed, not built; the scoring code applies to its output unchanged.
