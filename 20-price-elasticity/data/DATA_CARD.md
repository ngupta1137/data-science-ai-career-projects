# Data card: Retail Price Elasticity and Optimal Markup Pricing

## Source
Simulated weekly e-commerce transactions across diverse retail product categories with known true causal price elasticity and endogenous pricing. Generated in memory by `price_elasticity.data.generate_elasticity_data`.
- Deterministic simulation seed: 42 (training: 60 products across 100 weeks = 6,000 transactions; holdout test: 20 products across 100 weeks = 2,000 transactions).
- Cryptographic SHA-256 fingerprint pinned in `data/CHECKSUMS.txt`.

## Terms
Self-contained synthetic dataset generated by open-source code under the repository license (CC-BY-4.0).

**Data storage best practice:** Following industry best practices for algorithmic evaluation, raw synthetic CSV artifacts are not committed to git to prevent repository bloat and maintain clean version control. The dataset is generated on the fly via `python -m price_elasticity.train`.

## Shape
Documented by the simulation:
- 60 training products across 100 weeks (6,000 observations).
- 20 holdout test products across 100 weeks (2,000 observations).
- Product categories: Electronics (35%), Apparel (35%), Home & Living (30%).

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| product_id | Unique integer product catalog identifier |
| category | Stratified merchandise category (electronics, apparel, home_living) |
| week | 1-indexed transaction week (1 to 100) |
| wholesale_cost | Exogenous wholesale unit cost Z (supplier commodity and freight cost) |
| retail_price | Endogenous retail price P (pricing algorithms respond to demand shocks) |
| quantity_sold | Observed transaction sales volume Q |
| seasonality | Observable macro cyclical holiday and seasonal demand shifter X_1 |
| competitor_price | Observable log competitor average market price X_2 |
| demand_shock | Unobserved micro retail demand shock u (confounder creating endogeneity) |
| true_elasticity | Known ground truth causal price elasticity parameter beta |
| base_cost | Category baseline wholesale unit cost |

## Known quirks
- **Simultaneous price endogeneity:** Retail pricing algorithms mark up prices when unobserved demand shocks occur (u > 0), inducing positive correlation between price and error terms that attenuates OLS estimates toward zero.
- **Category heterogeneity:** Base sales velocity, baseline unit cost, and true elasticity differ across product categories, necessitating category-level fixed effect controls.
- **Log-log demand structure:** Constant elasticity specification ln Q = alpha + beta * ln P + gamma * X + u + eps.

## Not checked
- Cross-product cannibalization and multi-item portfolio cross-price elasticity.
- Dynamic customer purchase postponement and stockpiling behaviors.
- Supplier volume discounts and non-linear wholesale cost tiers.
