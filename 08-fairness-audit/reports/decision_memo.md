## Equal opportunity thresholds
Group thresholds tuned on dev give a test TPR gap of -0.084 (interval -0.127 to -0.038) at a utility cost of +0.0015 (-0.0045 to +0.0070). The post hoc study shows these thresholds average a gap of +0.002 on a large fresh set when tuned on 30000 dev rows, so the mitigation works on average. The test set cannot confirm it: with this many group B positives, the upper bound of the interval on the absolute gap is 0.127, above the 0.1 tolerance.

## What a wrong call costs
Signing off a policy that under-serves group B means qualified applicants are refused at a higher rate. Refusing to sign off a fine policy delays a launch. Neither can be priced here, because the costs in this project are assumptions.

## Where not to trust it
The worst-off group by true positive rate is group B. The data is simulated, there are only two groups, and equal opportunity is one of several definitions. The result says nothing about a real lender or employer.
