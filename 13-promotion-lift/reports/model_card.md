# Model card: promotion lift

Generated by `python -m promo_lift.report` from `results/`. Do not edit by hand.

## Intended use
Learning. A reference for measuring whether a store promotion made money, and why the usual shortcuts get the sign wrong. Not a promotion planning system.

## Data
One simulated campaign of 200 stores (100 promoted at random) over 52 weeks. Promotion weeks 30 to 33, post weeks 34 to 37, comparison weeks 22 to 29. See `data/DATA_CARD.md`.

## Assumptions
Margin 2 per unit on the promoted item, discount 0.6 per promoted unit, margin 1.5 on the sibling. All are assumptions.

## The traps, measured
- Before and after without control stores: estimate +56.9, bias +124.2 over campaigns. The promotion weeks fall on the seasonal peak, so a before-and-after view credits the season to the promotion.
- Counting only the promoted item in promotion weeks: estimate +17.5, bias +86.3. It misses cannibalisation (-37.2) and pull-forward (-49.1).
- Manager-picked stores: full difference-in-differences has bias +1.6 with random stores and +79.6 when the best-trending stores are picked, with coverage 0.068. When the stores with the best trend get the promotion, their growth is credited to it.

## Limits
- Everything is simulated. Store sizes, the season, the lift, cannibalisation, pull-forward and the margins are chosen by the author, so the sizes of the results describe this setup, not any retailer.
- One item, one sibling, one four-week promotion. No competing promotions, display or feature support, stock-outs, price ladders or halo on other categories.
- Stores are independent. Real stores share distribution centres, media and shoppers, which can leak a promotion into control stores.
- The promotion is randomised across stores in the headline campaign. The manager-picked variant shows what happens without that.
- Margins and the discount are assumptions. Profit here ignores supplier funding, which often pays part of the discount.
- Intervals assume stores are independent draws. They do not cover a mistaken choice of pre-period or post-period length.
