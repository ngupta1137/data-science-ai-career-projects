# Decision memo: newsgroup topic classifier

Generated by `python -m text_classification.report` from `results/`. Do not edit by hand.

## The question
Is a TF-IDF classifier accurate enough to route posts to topics as a first pass, and does a heavier model pay for itself over Naive Bayes?

## The rule, set before results were seen
Pass if both hold on the test set: (1) the lower bound of the headline model's 95% bootstrap interval on macro-F1 is above the majority-class baseline's macro-F1; (2) unless the headline is the Naive Bayes model itself, the lower bound of the 95% paired bootstrap interval on macro-F1 gain over Naive Bayes is above zero, so that any extra complexity is shown to pay for itself.

## The call: PASS
Headline tfidf_nb has test macro-F1 0.6761 (95% interval 0.6658 to 0.6864), against a majority baseline of 0.0050. Gain over Naive Bayes, 95% interval: not applicable (headline is Naive Bayes).

## What a wrong answer costs
A misrouted post lands with the wrong reviewers. The weakest classes and most confused pairs in the model card show where this happens. This project has no cost of a misroute, so no cost-weighted verdict is given.

## What would have fooled us
Leaving headers, footers, and quotes in the text scores 0.8252, a change of +0.1491. That score measures who posted, not what the post says. Report the cleaned number.
