# Model card: text classification

Generated by `python -m text_classification.report` from `results/metrics.json`. Do not edit by hand.

## Intended use
Learning. A reference for evaluating a multi-class text classifier and for spotting shortcut features. Not for moderating or routing real messages.

## Data
20 newsgroups, 18,846 posts. See `data/DATA_CARD.md`.

## Split
Fit 9,051 posts, validation 2,263 (stratified share of the training set, used to choose the model), test 7,532 (official test set, later in time than the training set).

## Results
Macro-F1 is the unweighted mean of per-class F1.

Headline: **tfidf_nb**, chosen by validation macro-F1. Test macro-F1 0.6761, 95% interval 0.6658 to 0.6864 (document bootstrap, 1000 resamples). Majority baseline 0.0050. Gain over Naive Bayes, 95% interval: not applicable (headline is Naive Bayes).

## The traps, measured
- Metadata kept: test macro-F1 0.8252 with headers, footers, and quotes versus 0.6761 without (+0.1491). 0.0% of the model's top features never appear in the cleaned text. Top tokens for alt.atheism: the, of, keith, that, to, edu, you, is. Headers, footers, and quoted replies identify the poster and thread, not the topic.
- Vectorizer fitted on test text: 0.6795 versus 0.6761 honest (+0.0035). Fitting vocabulary and IDF on test text lets test statistics shape the features.

## Where it fails
Weakest classes by F1: talk.religion.misc (0.213), alt.atheism (0.479), talk.politics.misc (0.501), comp.os.ms-windows.misc (0.595), talk.politics.guns (0.639). Most confused pairs:
- talk.religion.misc predicted as soc.religion.christian: 114 documents
- talk.politics.misc predicted as talk.politics.guns: 96 documents
- alt.atheism predicted as soc.religion.christian: 87 documents
- comp.os.ms-windows.misc predicted as comp.sys.ibm.pc.hardware: 64 documents
- comp.windows.x predicted as comp.graphics: 46 documents

Empty documents after cleaning: 215 (2.9% of test). Full tables are in `results/error_analysis.json`.

## Limits
- The interval resamples documents as if independent. Crossposted and quoted posts are not independent, so it is somewhat too narrow.
- Posts are from 1990s Usenet. Vocabulary and topics do not transfer to current text.
- Models are fitted on the fit rows only, not refitted on fit plus validation, so test scores are slightly conservative.
- A transformer comparison is not built in this version. See the project README.
- The metadata trap result is for the headline model and these hyperparameters. Other models may leak more or less.
