# Decision memo: weekly demand forecast

Generated by `python -m demand_forecast.report` from `results/`. Do not edit by hand.

## The question
Can a 7 day ahead daily demand forecast from calendar facts and past demand beat the best simple baseline?

## The rule, set before results were seen
Pass if the lower bound of the 95% block-bootstrap interval on the test MAE gap (best baseline MAE minus headline model MAE) is above zero. The headline model and the best baseline are both chosen on the tuning folds, never on the test folds.

## The call: FAIL
Headline ridge has test MAE 984.6 (95% interval 778.0 to 1236.5). Best baseline baseline_mean_28 has test MAE 961.0. The gap in MAE, baseline minus model, has a 95% interval of -95.9 to 41.6.

## What a wrong answer costs
A forecast error of about 985 rentals a day is the typical miss at this horizon. Whether that matters depends on fleet and staffing costs this project does not have.

## Where not to trust it
Worst weekday: 6. Weather is not an input, so weather-driven swings are missed. Two years of one city is not enough to trust a year-over-year pattern.

## Not done
No weather forecast inputs. No prediction intervals. No hierarchical or multi-location forecasting. No cost model for over- and under-forecasting.
