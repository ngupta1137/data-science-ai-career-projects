## What this means
The two-part pricing model passes all three pre-registered decision checks. It provides significant risk segmentation across the portfolio (Gini 95% lower bound above 0.25), improves discrimination over a single compound Tweedie regression, and keeps the top-risk decile profitable at a loss ratio of 0.6777 (comfortably below the 0.95 cap and 1.0 break-even limit).

## Where this is fragile
The model assumes claims severity follows a Gamma distribution with moderate variance. In reality, bodily injury and catastrophic casualty claims often follow heavy-tailed Pareto distributions where a tiny fraction of claims dominate aggregate losses. Furthermore, exposure duration is assumed uninformative; in commercial auto or personal lines, short-term policies may experience adverse selection beyond their duration.

## What a wrong call costs
Underpricing high-risk drivers induces adverse selection: competitors price them accurately, leaving this portfolio concentrated in unprofitable policies (top-decile loss ratio exceeds 1.61 under flat rates). Overpricing low-risk drivers drives profitable policyholders to competitors (decile 1 loss ratio 0.19).
