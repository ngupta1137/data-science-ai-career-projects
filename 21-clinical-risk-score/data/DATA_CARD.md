# Data Card: 30-Day Post-Discharge Clinical Adverse Event Risk

> [!NOTE]
> No patient data is used in this repository. All clinical observations are synthetically generated. Nothing herein constitutes medical advice, clinical guidance, or regulatory determination.

## Source
Simulated patient inpatient discharge records across three regional hospital facilities with parameterized baseline risk, clinical acuity, and 30-day post-discharge outcomes. Generated deterministically in memory by `clinical_risk.data.generate_clinical_data`.
- Training cohort: 6,000 patient discharges (seed: 42).
- Holdout test cohort: 3,000 patient discharges (seed: 142).
- Cryptographic SHA-256 fingerprint pinned in `data/CHECKSUMS.txt`.

## Terms
Self-contained synthetic dataset generated by open-source code under the repository license (CC-BY-4.0).

**Data storage best practice:** Following industry best practices for algorithmic evaluation, raw synthetic CSV files are not committed to git to prevent repository bloat and maintain clean version control. The dataset is generated on the fly via `python -m clinical_risk.train`.

## Shape
Documented by the simulation:
- 6,000 training patient discharge records.
- 3,000 holdout test patient discharge records.
- Overall adverse event / readmission prevalence: ~21.2% train, ~21.6% test.
- Facilities: Tertiary Academic Center (45%), Community Regional Hospital (40%), Rural Critical Access Hospital (15%).

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| patient_id | Unique integer patient admission identifier |
| hospital_site | Categorical facility identifier (tertiary_academic, community_regional, rural_access) |
| age | Patient age at discharge in years (Gaussian distribution, bounded 18 to 102) |
| prior_admissions | Inpatient hospital admissions in the preceding 12 months (Poisson distribution) |
| comorbidity_charlson | Weighted Charlson comorbidity index capturing chronic conditions (0 to 12) |
| length_of_stay | Inpatient index hospital length of stay in days (log-normal / exponential distribution) |
| polypharmacy_med_count | Number of prescribed discharge medications (polypharmacy indicator) |
| abnormal_lab_count | Number of critical abnormal laboratory findings during index admission |
| social_vulnerability_index | Area deprivation and social vulnerability index score (0 to 1) |
| true_risk_probability | Latent ground truth 30-day post-discharge adverse event risk probability |
| readmitted_30d | Observed binary adverse outcome or unplanned readmission within 30 days (0 or 1) |

## Known Quirks
- **Hospital facility case-mix shift:** Tertiary academic centers handle higher surgical and chronic disease complexity, yielding 27.9% observed prevalence, while rural critical access hospitals observe 11.0% prevalence. Models lacking facility controls over-predict rural risk.
- **Class imbalance:** Adverse events occur in approximately one-fifth of discharges, tempting standard medical ML workflows into applying positive class rebalancing that severely degrades probability calibration.
- **Asymmetric decision utility:** The transitional care nurse contact costs 150 USD whereas preventing a hospital readmission saves 1,000 USD net, establishing an operational decision threshold at pt = 0.15.

## Not Checked
- Competing risk of in-hospital or immediate post-discharge mortality.
- Time-varying physiological telemetry during index hospitalization.
- Unstructured nursing notes and physician discharge summaries.
- Social determinants of health (housing instability, pharmacy desert indices).
